FLIK O2O

Client FLIK Role Product Designer Year 2025 Tags O2O · Checkout · Product Design

FLIK was growing fast online, but when it came to offline stores, there was a problem: no EDC machines, no easy way to link in-store purchases to their digital system. Cashiers had to manually collect customer info: messy, slow, and nobody liked it. We needed to fix it, fast.

Goals

  • Create a seamless, fast flow for cashiers with minimal friction.
  • Activate shoppers online immediately after an offline transaction.
  • Motivate cashiers to consistently collect and link shopper data.

Cashiers are super busy. If it's not simple and rewarding, they won't use it.

Introducing FLIK O2O

FLIK O2O connects what happens in-store with what happens online via FLIK QRIS. Shoppers scan a unique QR code at checkout with any mobile banking app or e-wallet, instantly linking their data so merchants can track purchases and grow with a connected experience.

Result

Launched within 3–4 weeks at Erigo, Teras Jahe, and Emado's Shawarma.
