FLIK O2O: Where Offline Meets Online

Turned FLIK's messy offline-to-online purchase tracking into a working O2O checkout flow, connecting in-store sales to their digital system without EDC machines.

O2O Checkout Product Design Product Designer · 2025

The Situation

FLIK was growing fast online, but when it came to offline stores, there was a problem: no EDC machines, no easy way to link in-store purchases to their digital system. Cashiers had to manually collect customer info: messy, slow, and nobody liked it. We needed to fix it, fast.

Goals

  • Create a seamless, fast flow for cashiers with minimal friction.
  • Activate shoppers online immediately after an offline transaction.
  • Motivate cashiers to consistently collect and link shopper data.

What We Found

Field visits and cashier interviews surfaced three blunt truths: cashiers are super busy and don't have time for anything complicated, manual data collection produces lots of mistakes, and if it's not simple and rewarding, they won't use it.

Introducing FLIK O2O

FLIK O2O connects what happens in-store with what happens online via FLIK QRIS. Shoppers scan a unique QR code at checkout with any mobile banking app or e-wallet, instantly linking their data so merchants can track purchases and grow with a connected experience.

High-Fidelity Design

From the stakeholder meetings, we mapped a user flow for offline transactions and built a simple cashier UI on top of it.

Login Flow for Cashier

If the Cashier Web App is opened without logging in, the user is redirected to the login page. After entering a valid email and password, a session starts and the cashier is taken to the shopper's phone-number input. Login also tracks data and gives incentives: FLIK Points worth 10% per transaction, up to Rp10,000.

Input Data (Phone Number)

The shopper phone-number page is the first step: the app auto-formats the number, removing a leading "62" or "0" so it starts with "8." Once valid, the app checks whether the shopper is new or existing, then routes to the right detail page.

Result

Launched within 3–4 weeks at Erigo, Teras Jahe, and Emado's Shawarma. FLIK O2O generated over Rp6 billion in GMV in its first month, pushing FLIK's total GMV from Rp10B to Rp16B: a 60% increase.
