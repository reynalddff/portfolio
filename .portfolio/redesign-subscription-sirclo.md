## Identified Issues in Subscription

The low number of merchants subscribing indicates that there are areas in the subscription feature on the 'Tagihan dan Paket' page that need improvement. Several factors contribute to this issue, including:

- Lack of clear and accessible information about the benefits and features of each plan on the 'View Packages' and 'Add On' pages.
- Missing details about ongoing subscription promotions when merchants try to subscribe or renew paid packages.
- Confusion in the subscription process flow, which needs to be aligned for a better user experience.

To effectively address these concerns, we recommend taking the following actions:

1. **Make information clearer:** Provide comprehensive and easy-to-understand details about the benefits and features of each plan on the 'View Packages' and 'Add On' pages. Make sure merchants have a clear understanding of the value they will receive.
2. **Highlight subscription promotions:** Clearly communicate any ongoing promotions or discounts during the self-subscription and package renewal process. This will increase awareness and encourage merchants to subscribe independently.
3. **Simplify the subscription process:** Streamline the subscription flows to create a seamless experience for merchants. Evaluate the current process and identify areas that might confuse users, then make the necessary adjustments to improve the user journey.

By implementing these improvements, our goal is to make it easier for merchants to subscribe independently, enhance the user experience, and increase the adoption of our subscription packages.

## What We Aim to Achieve

To tackle the issue of fewer merchants subscribing and make the subscription feature better, our main aim is to boost subscription rates. Our specific target is to achieve a whopping 50% increase in the number of monthly self-subscriptions.

By putting our efforts into achieving this goal, we strive to bring in more merchant subscriptions and make our subscription feature a big success.

## Fixing the Subscription Feature

Based on the issues we've identified, it's crucial to improve the information and visuals on the 'Tagihan dan Paket' page. We need to discuss and plan further development to enhance the user experience and flow, considering the technical limitations. Since we have an urgent need for facilitation and some limitations to address, here are the planned improvements for these pages:

1. **'Lihat Paket' Menu:** This menu will provide information about the currently used package. It will also include a billing sub-menu with payment details for merchants subscribing to a paid package. You'll find clear buttons to subscribe or renew your plans, as well as a button to guide you to the domain purchase page.
2. **'Add On' Menu:** This menu will showcase both packages (products/plans offered by SIRCLO Store) and add-ons (additional products). It will be a one-stop shop for users to subscribe to a paid package. Here's what you'll find in this menu:
   - **Packages:** Choose from a variety of subscription packages with multiple benefits tailored to your needs.
   - **Add On:** Get additional features/products that can be purchased separately, such as custom domains (no need for a package subscription).

By implementing these changes, our aim is to make information more accessible and clear, simplify the subscription process, and provide a better overall experience for merchants.

## Let's Get Creative! Generating Awesome Ideas Together!

### Getting to Know Our Rivals!

As part of our design team's research, we've conducted a competitor analysis on several companies that operate in a similar field and offer services with a membership system. We focused on three direct competitors, namely LummoShop, TokoTalk (Plugo), and Jubelio, as well as three indirect competitors, namely Figma, Miro, and Wix.

Through this analysis, we discovered valuable insights that can help us improve our own services. Here's what we found:

1. **Membership Status Information:** Our competitors' pages provide clear information about the user's membership status. This feature allows users to easily understand their current subscription level.
2. **Membership Status History:** The competitor pages also offer access to the user's membership status history. This feature enables users to track their subscription changes and understand their membership journey.
3. **Plan Comparison Table:** One notable feature we observed is the presence of a plan comparison table on the competitor pages. This table allows users to compare the features and benefits of different subscription plans side by side.
4. **Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs):** Competitors also provide a dedicated section for frequently asked questions. This helps users find answers to common queries and enhances their overall experience.

By conducting this competitor analysis, we gain valuable insights into the features and functionalities that are appreciated by users in our industry. This knowledge will guide us in improving our own services and creating a better experience for our valued users.
